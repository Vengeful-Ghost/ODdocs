Delete your account

Oblivion Descent · Last updated 10 November 2026

You can delete your Oblivion Descent account and its game data at any time, in the game or by email.

In the game

  1. Open Settings from the main menu and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  3. If the game asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in and go straight back to Delete Account (Google only lets a recent sign-in delete an account).

Without the game

Email stivenstregalev@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account and your username. We may ask you to confirm the request, and we delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username and sign-in.
  • Your saved runs, statistics, Souls, Soul Altar upgrades, heroes and Wardrobe.
  • The players you follow, and your Daily Run and Weekly Challenge results.
  • The global leaderboard drops you at its next daily update.

What is kept

  • Anonymous run records used to balance the game. They are not linked to your account or username and expire after 12 months.
  • Crash reports and usage analytics, which are not linked to your account; Google Firebase removes them under its own retention settings.
  • Progress from offline play never reaches our servers: uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it.
